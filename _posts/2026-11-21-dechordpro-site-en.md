## DeChordPro Support

This page provides feature information and support for the app.

### About
DeChordPro listens to any song and shows you every chord on a clean bar-by-beat grid. Import audio from your files — or hum a melody — and get the full chord progression in seconds, with key, tempo and time signature. Practice with fingering charts for guitar, piano and ukulele, slow-down, transpose, capo and section loops. Pro members can split songs into stems in the cloud.

**Key features:**

• **Chord grid** – One row per bar, one pad per beat, playhead chasing as you listen.

• **Fingering charts** – Guitar, piano and ukulele diagrams with capo support.

• **Practice tools** – Tempo change without pitch shift, transpose, tap-to-loop sections, 4/8/16-bar jumps.

• **Cloud stems** – Split into vocals, drums, bass and more, then remix the balance (Pro).

• **Every device** – Built native for iPhone, iPad, Mac and iPhone Duo; one purchase follows your Apple ID.

### Privacy
Chord analysis runs entirely on-device. Cloud stem separation only processes the file you choose. Diagnostics (crash/performance reports) are collected via Apple MetricKit without identifying you.
