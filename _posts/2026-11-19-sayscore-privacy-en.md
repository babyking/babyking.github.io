## SayScore Privacy Policy

SayScore is a private reading coach: record yourself reading a short piece aloud, and the app scores your delivery on-device — pronunciation, pacing, pauses, and filler words — then builds practice around your weak spots.
The app contains no ads and no analytics SDKs, and your data is never used for advertising.

### Data & Privacy
- **Fully on-device**: Audio recording and all speech analysis run locally on your device, including the on-device speech-recognition model. Your voice is never uploaded — there is no server component.
- **No accounts**: The app requires no sign-up and collects no personal information.
- **Local data**: Recordings, scores, history, and practice progress are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.
- **Permissions**: Microphone access is used only to record your reading takes; Speech Recognition access is used only to transcribe them on-device. Both are requested in context and can be revoked in Settings.
- We do not sell or share user data with third parties.
