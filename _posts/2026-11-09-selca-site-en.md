## Selca - Korean Film Selfie Camera Support

This page provides feature information and support for the app.

### About
Selca is a Korean-style filter selfie camera: open and shoot, live filter preview,
one tap to a finished photo. No ads, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases —
pay once and every filter is yours forever.

**Key features:**

• **Live filter viewfinder** – 16 Korean-style filters (milk / butter / cool / blossom / princess / vintage / iPhone 7…) previewed in real time. What you see is what you get.

• **Dial-in control** – Filter strength 0–100, brightness ±50, soft glow 0–100, tuned with a precise tick-mark slider.

• **Before/After compare** – Drag the split line while shooting or editing to compare the original with the filtered result.

• **Remix old photos** – Import from your photo library, apply the same filters and effects, and save back to your library.

• **Paid upfront** – One purchase. No ads, no subscriptions, no feature locks.
