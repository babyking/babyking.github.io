## Selca Privacy Policy

Selca - Korean Film Selfie Camera is a paid-upfront filter camera app.
The app contains no ads and no analytics SDKs, and your data is never used for advertising.

### Data & Privacy
- **Camera & Photo Library access**: used only to take photos and to read or save the pictures you choose. All filter processing happens locally on your device — your photos are never uploaded to any server.
- **No personal data collection**: no accounts, no personal information collected for marketing.
- System logs, if any, are used solely for debugging and stability analysis.
- We do not sell or share user data with third parties.
