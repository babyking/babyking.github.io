## TingBei Support

This page provides feature information and support for the app.

### About
TingBei turns your study materials into repeatable audio: import a document, scan a page, or paste text — it is automatically recognized, cleaned up, and converted into audio with sentence-level subtitles. With repeat playback, read-along, and a sleep timer, commutes and spare moments become memorization time.

**Key features:**

• **Three ways to import** – Files (PDF/DOCX/EPUB/TXT), camera scan (OCR), or paste text. A built-in sample lets you try the full pipeline instantly.

• **Smart pipeline** – Automatic document parsing, OCR for scanned pages, text cleanup, and chapter splitting. Preview the recognized text before conversion.

• **Sentence-level subtitles** – Generated audio comes with per-sentence subtitle highlighting; tap any sentence to jump straight to it. Supports repeat, playback speed (0.5x–2x), and ±15s seek.

• **Memorization toolkit** – Bookmarks, chapter list, sleep timer, and read-along recording, designed for listen-and-recite learning.

• **Built-in listening packs** – Sample audio including classical poetry, English essays, and exam points plays out of the box.

• **Four languages** – Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese.

### Privacy
Imported documents are stored on our servers only while being processed (source files deleted within 24 hours, generated files within 48 hours). No accounts, no ads, no analytics SDKs.
