## TingBei Privacy Policy

TingBei turns your documents, scanned pages, or pasted text into audio with sentence-level subtitles, designed for listening-based memorization.
The app contains no ads and no analytics SDKs, and your data is never used for advertising.

### Data & Privacy
- **Document processing**: To perform OCR, text cleanup, and speech synthesis, imported documents are uploaded to our servers. This is required for the app's core functionality.
- **Automatic deletion**: Uploaded source documents are automatically deleted from our servers within 24 hours; generated audio and subtitle files within 48 hours. We do not retain your content long-term.
- **No accounts**: The app requires no sign-up. We only use an anonymous device identifier to manage conversion quota; it is not linked to any personal identity.
- **Local data**: Playback progress, bookmarks, and your library are stored only on your device and never uploaded.
- **Permissions**: Photo library and camera access are used only to import material images you choose to convert; microphone access is used only for the read-along recording feature.
- We do not sell or share user data with third parties.
