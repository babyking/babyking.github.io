## BudgetMeals Privacy Policy

BudgetMeals plans a week of dinners around your grocery budget: set a weekly amount and household size, and the app builds a full plan of recipes plus an aisle-grouped shopping list.
The app contains no ads and no analytics SDKs, and your data is never used for advertising.

### Data & Privacy
- **Fully on-device**: Meal planning, recipe catalog, price data, and your plans all live locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded — there is no server component.
- **No accounts**: The app requires no sign-up and collects no personal information.
- **Local data**: Your plans, shopping lists, checked items, and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.
- **No special permissions**: BudgetMeals does not use the microphone, camera, photos, contacts, or location.
- We do not sell or share user data with third parties.
