## AuralGym Privacy Policy

AuralGym is a bite-size ear-training app: short daily lessons in intervals, chords, rhythm, and sing-along pitch scoring — built for musicians who want three focused minutes a day.
The app contains no ads and no analytics SDKs, and your data is never used for advertising.

### Data & Privacy
- **Fully on-device**: Lesson playback, pitch detection, and scoring all run locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded — there is no server component.
- **Microphone**: The mic is used only during sing-along lessons to measure pitch in real time. Audio is analyzed on-device and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.
- **No accounts**: The app requires no sign-up and collects no personal information.
- **Local data**: Lesson progress, streaks, and preferences are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.
- We do not sell or share user data with third parties.
