## BudgetMeals Support

This page provides feature information and support for the app.

### About
$50 a week of groceries, planned. Tell BudgetMeals your weekly budget and household size, and it builds a full week of dinners from a price-aware recipe catalog — then turns the plan into an aisle-grouped shopping list that stays inside your number.

**Key features:**

• **Budget-first planning** – Set your weekly amount and household size; the engine picks recipes whose estimated basket cost fits.

• **A whole week in one pass** – Seven dinners with servings, diet and allergen filters applied to every slot.

• **Aisle list that checks itself** – Ingredients merge into a store-ordered list with pack-vs-need math and a running total as you tick items off.

• **Receipt-style counter** – A punched price tag tracks estimated spend against your budget as you plan.

• **Generous free tier** – Browse the full recipe catalog and build one example week free. Pro unlocks unlimited rebuilds, per-meal swaps, and instant re-planning when you change budget or filters.

### Privacy
Everything runs on-device: no accounts, no uploads, no ads, no analytics SDKs.
