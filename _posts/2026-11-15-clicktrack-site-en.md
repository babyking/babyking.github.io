## ClickTrack

**ClickTrack: Practice To The Real Song**

ClickTrack turns any song into your practice room. Import a track, remove the drums, and play along to a click that follows the song's real tempo map — including every accelerando, ritardando and tempo change — instead of forcing your music into a static BPM.

**Key Features:**

1. **Stem Separation:** Remove the drum track (or isolate stems) from any imported song, so you can take the drummer's seat yourself.
2. **Real Tempo Maps:** Automatic beat detection preserves the song's true variable tempo — no flattening, no rounding to a fixed BPM.
3. **Editable Beat Grid:** Move, nudge, insert or delete beats, set downbeats and time signatures, with full undo/redo. The beat map is yours, not a black box.
4. **Count-In Anywhere:** Start the click before the song, before the drums come in, or at any point you choose — perfect for entries that are hard to catch.
5. **Eight Click Timbres:** From classic metronome to woodblock and stick sounds, with independent volume for click, song and stems.
6. **Practice Tools:** Seamless A–B looping, ±12 semitone pitch shift, 50–150% speed, and background playback.
7. **Export With Tempo Intact:** Export your work with the real tempo map preserved for your DAW or bandmates.

**Who is it for:**

Drummers learning songs note-for-note, drummers recording covers, guitarists and bassists practicing to the original track, and anyone who needs a click that actually follows the music.

Applicable scene:
Song learning, cover recording, tempo/feel training, click track production for live performance, band rehearsal preparation, music education.
