## AuralGym

**Three focused minutes a day for your ears.** Bite-size lessons in intervals, chords, rhythm — plus a sing-along coach that scores your pitch in real time, entirely on your device.

### Features
- Daily bite-size lessons: intervals → chords → rhythm → sing-along
- Real-time pitch scoring (on-device, nothing uploaded)
- Free toolbox: tuning fork, metronome, interval ear tester
- Streaks and daily rituals to keep your ears sharp

### AuralGym Pro
- Yearly $29.99 · Monthly $5.99 · both with a 7-day free trial
- Unlocks all lessons, sing-along scoring, unlimited practice
- Manage or cancel anytime in App Store → Subscriptions
