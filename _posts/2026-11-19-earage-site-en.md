## EarAge Support

This page provides feature information and support for the app.

### About
How old are your ears? EarAge plays pure tones that climb in frequency — you tap each time you hear one, and the app estimates your ear age from the highest frequency you can still catch, matched against age-group norms. It takes about a minute, works offline, and gives you a shareable result card.

**Key features:**

• **One-minute test** – Pure-tone sweep from everyday frequencies up to 20 kHz; tap "heard it" as each tone becomes audible.

• **Ear-age score** – Your highest audible frequency is mapped to an age-matched norm scale, rendered as a shareable card.

• **Catch checks** – Occasional silent rounds keep the result honest, so the score reflects real hearing, not tapping rhythm.

• **Pro: your hearing over time** – The core test is always free. Pro unlocks retest history, the full norm-scale comparison, and extra card styles.

### Privacy
Everything runs on-device: no accounts, no uploads, no ads, no analytics SDKs.
