## HearAge Privacy Policy

HearAge is a hearing-age test: the app plays a series of pure tones at rising frequencies, you tap when you hear one, and it estimates your "ear age" against age-matched norms — with a shareable result card.
The app contains no ads and no analytics SDKs, and your data is never used for advertising.

### Data & Privacy
- **Fully on-device**: Tone generation, scoring, and results all run locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded — there is no server component.
- **No accounts**: The app requires no sign-up and collects no personal information.
- **Local data**: Test results, retest history, and preferences are stored only on your device. Deleting the app removes them.
- **No special permissions**: HearAge only plays audio through your speaker or headphones. It does not use the microphone, camera, photos, contacts, or location.
- We do not sell or share user data with third parties.
