## VocalCoach Support

This page provides feature information and support for the app.

### About
A reading coach that lives entirely on your iPhone. Pick a piece, tap the mic, read it aloud for 30–60 seconds — VocalCoach scores the take on-device and shows you exactly which words, sounds, and pauses to work on.

**Key features:**

• **Word-level heat map** – Every word in your take is marked clean, weak, or missed, so you see the trouble spots at a glance.

• **Honest scoring** – Pronunciation plus pacing, pauses, and filler words, scored by an on-device model. When a language can't be fully scored, the report says so and shows the metrics it can stand behind.

• **Daily plan** – Short drills built around your weak sounds, refreshed from your recent takes.

• **Your journey** – A week of dots and a trend curve turn "am I improving?" into a picture.

• **Private by design** – Recording and analysis never leave the device. It's just between you and the coach.

### Privacy
Recording, transcription, and scoring all run on-device: no accounts, no uploads, no ads, no analytics SDKs.
